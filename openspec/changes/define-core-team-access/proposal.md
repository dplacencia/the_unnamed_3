## Why

Merchants need to understand who belongs to their team, what each person can do
and which invitations are still active. Core distinguishes membership, scoped
role assignments and authority to manage invitations.

## What Changes

- Define a compact team workspace for member, role and invitation management.
- Preview scoped role assignment, role creation, last-Owner refusal and email-only
  invitation actions using the decisions already recorded for Core.
- Review preselected roles in a separate invitation preview.
- Exercise the views in Storybook in Spanish, English, Catalan, Galician and Basque.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `core-team-access` — Review Core team, role and invitation views.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

The preview uses deterministic Core responses. Live integration needs the
published role, roster, delegation and invitation administration contracts.
