# Clarify the Owner's first steps in Inicio

## Why

The current Owner preview puts trial information before a generic next-step
button. It can also select the first Area when more than one qualifies. The
merchant should be able to see what they can do next and choose the Area when
there is a real choice.

## What changes

- Compare the current Inicio and a proposed TailAdmin card with one useful next
  action and a short explanation.
- Use confirmed Site, Area and Inventory facts to guide stock setup. Show the
  named Area and Site for a sole eligible Area. Reuse the Area list when several
  qualify.
- Keep authorized Core work available when Inventory is paused or its access
  cannot be checked.

The comparison uses synthetic business data in Storybook. Review of the
proposed design follows the preview. The established business rules in the
[Owner journey](../assemble-owner-journey/specs/owner-journey/spec.md) govern it.
