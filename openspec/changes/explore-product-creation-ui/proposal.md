# Explore simple and advanced product creation

## Why

The user chose a simple default with complexity exposed on request. A concrete
comparison will help them judge that experience before adopting a UI mechanism.

## What Changes

- Prepare an interactive Spanish comparison of one product draft in simple and
  advanced views, adapted from the supplied TailAdmin form primitives.
- Record sources, proposals and verification in [design.md](design.md) and
  [tasks.md](tasks.md).

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None. This is an exploratory prototype and documentation change.

### Modified Capabilities

None. No product-level behavior or existing requirement is changed.

## Impact

Files are confined to this change and a discovery pointer in root `AGENTS.md`.
No app scaffold, package installation, backend connection, real product creation,
deployment, archive or commit. Supplied ZIPs and sibling repositories remain
untouched. `skip_specs: true` declares the absence of specification deltas.

## Authority and status

On 2026-11-11, after the assistant recommended comparing the same product-creation
workflow in simple and advanced views using TailAdmin, the user replied:
“ok, go ahead”. This authorizes a reviewable concept, not adoption of its layout,
two permanent tiers, preference persistence, branding or production architecture.

The [foundation scope](../establish-frontend-foundation/specs/frontend-scope/spec.md)
and [values](../establish-frontend-foundation/specs/product-values/spec.md) still
govern. This change remains unarchived. The revised concept has user acceptance
as a design reference, within the scope recorded below.

The first feedback accepted the advanced concept's appearance and identified
quantity-step information as excessive in the simple view. The bounded revision
and exact feedback are recorded in [design.md](design.md#user-feedback-remove-advanced-information-from-the-simple-view).

After viewing the revised comparison, the user said “That looks ok”. This accepts
the revised simple/advanced presentation concept: essentials in the simple view,
technical details available on request, and the advanced concept as shown. It
does not authorize production implementation or settle global modes, preference
persistence, architecture, branding or other unresolved product choices.
